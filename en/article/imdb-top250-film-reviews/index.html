IMDB Top 250 Quick Reviews

About 1004 wordsAbout 3 min

Classic MediaHobbies

2025-01-30

I only comment on the films I have watched and genuinely liked, following IMDB's default ranking.

The Shawshank Redemption

IMDB No. 1. That alone says enough.

The Godfather

Watched it in the AV room next to the university library and sat through the full three hours.

The Dark Knight

I listened to the track RISEN FROM DARKNESS on repeat for a long time.

The Lord of the Rings: The Return of the King

The Rohirrim charge in the final battle is pure adrenaline.

Forrest Gump

Life is the process of holding on to your own box of chocolates.

Inception

The dream-within-a-dream concept was stunning. About the spinning top ending: I'm on Team IT STOPS.

Star Wars: The Empire Strikes Back

One of the few sci-fi blockbusters I watched as a kid. The AT-ATs stayed with me.

The Matrix

I was dazzled by the effects at first. Only later did I realize how deep the series allegory is.

Interstellar

I am always drawn to time, the cosmos, and black holes. The score is superb, and the video-message scene is deeply moving.

Se7en

Brad Pitt was very cool. The film carries a heavy religious atmosphere; later works like True Detective feel influenced by it.

Saving Private Ryan

I've rewatched the D-Day landing opening dozens of times.

City of God

Brutal.

Terminator 2: Judgment Day

Hasta la vista, baby.

Léon: The Professional

I like Gary Oldman's performance even more.

The Usual Suspects

Kevin Spacey played villains brilliantly. It's a shame how his later path narrowed.

Casablanca

_Of all the gin joints in all the towns in all the world, she walks into mine._

Alien

I've watched Alien 1-4, the two prequels, Romulus, and all kinds of Alien vs. Predator spin-offs.

Memento

A mind-bending structure. I needed other people's explanations to truly understand it.

Raiders of the Lost Ark

Dr. Indiana Jones was basically my first teacher who sparked interest in archaeology.

Avengers: Infinity War

For me, the Avengers series is the ceiling of superhero movies.

Your Name

Warm visuals, beautiful songs, unforgettable memories.

Heat

For a while I really loved Al Pacino's style.

L.A. Confidential

A classic Hollywood cops-and-robbers story.

Shutter Island

The first film that truly made me respect Leonardo DiCaprio's acting.

Top Gun: Maverick

To fly like Maverick is probably every man's dream.

The Sixth Sense

The twist at the end is unforgettable.

No Country for Old Men

Watched it under the covers on a snowy day back in 2008. A very happy memory.

The Thing

A bit old, but the horror atmosphere is excellent.

Harry Potter and the Deathly Hallows: Part 2

It's worth reading professional critiques of the whole series, or you'll miss many details and contexts.

Mad Max: Fury Road

A wild post-apocalyptic world. The female lead is incredibly cool.

Memories of Murder

It changed my stereotypes about Korean cinema. Song Kang-ho is stunning at the end.

Pirates of the Caribbean: The Curse of the Black Pearl

Free-spirited, morally gray, and effortlessly stylish.

Groundhog Day

Every day can be brand new.
