> Author: Zhu Jianrong. Published on April 21, 2020 in *Today's Jingde*.

In the east corner of the slow city\[^1], clouds drift high,
Terraced slopes gather crimson mist layer by layer.
In the mountain, a rare encounter among ten thousand,
Tea girls pick red azaleas from the leaves they lay.

Notes:

\[^1]: At the "International Cittaslow 2016 China Annual Conference," Jingyang Town of Jingde County, Xuancheng City successfully joined the International Cittaslow network and received official accreditation. It became the first in Anhui Province and the sixth in China. (https://www.ahjd.gov.cn/News/show/638264.html)
