> Author: Zhu Jianrong. Published on July 11, 2022 in *Today's Jingde*.

The ancient East greets the sunrise early,

The Asian Games torch flares toward the sky.

Not everyone knows Hangzhou's Mid-Autumn moon,

How many truly understand the Qiantang tide?
