> Author: Zhu Jianrong. Published on July 11, 2022 in *Today's Jingde*.

At Ningguo Martyrs' Cemetery, we pay our respects,

By Chujiatan, landscapes stir the heart.

Xicun's countryside shines with quiet beauty,

To comfort the spirits of the fallen above.
