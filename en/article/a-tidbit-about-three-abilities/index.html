A Survival Guide for the Next 10 Years: Three Skills That Decide Whether You Get Eliminated

Introduction: Your Job Is Being Redefined by the Future

Have you ever wondered what work will look like in 10 years?

  • Remote work: around 40% of companies have moved permanently to remote or hybrid models (Gartner).
  • Video dominates: more than 1 billion hours of video are watched on YouTube every day; TikTok has over 2 billion users.
  • Specialization: by 2030, 85% of jobs will require new skill combinations (World Economic Forum).

The future workplace is no longer a fixed "9-to-5" pattern. It is a new test of domain expertise, writing, and video creation. Without these skills, you may be eliminated by the times.

1. Domain Expertise: How Deep Is Your Moat?

Why is expertise hard currency?

  • Data: LinkedIn reports that 87% of employers treat "professional skills" as the top hiring factor.
  • Reality: Tesla engineers have a median salary over $150k, while many general roles average around $50k.

How to build expertise

  • Pick a niche: data analysis, AI, UX design, etc.
  • Go deep: 1 hour a day for a year is enough to surpass most peers.
  • Quantify outcomes: e.g., "reduced operating cost by 20% by optimizing an algorithm."

If you have no specialization, you may be replaced by AI or by younger specialists.

2. Writing: In Remote Work, Your Pen Is Your Voice

Why writing matters more than face-to-face talk

  • Remote trend: by 2025, 36.2% of workers will be long-term remote (Upwork).
  • Efficiency: a clear project doc can save 50% of communication time.

How to improve writing

  • Use structured expression: pyramid principle (conclusion first).
  • Practice: write weekly summaries or analyses; after a year, your logic will sharpen.
  • Use tools: Grammarly, Notion, etc.

If you cannot write clearly, remote work can marginalize you.

3. Video Creation: Let a Billion People See Your Ideas

Why video is a super传播 tool

  • 90% of users prefer learning about a new product via video (Wyzowl).
  • Short video ads convert ~30% better than text/image ads.

How to learn video creation

  • Learn tools: CapCut, Premiere, Final Cut Pro.
  • Study viral content structure and rhythm; imitate, then innovate.
  • Output consistently: one short video per week; in a year you might build an audience.

If you cannot express through video, your ideas may drown in the information flood.

Conclusion: The Future Is Here—Are You Ready?

The future workplace is no longer about endurance; it is about ability.

  • Expertise keeps you competitive.
  • Writing helps you stand out in remote collaboration.
  • Video makes your ideas visible.

Start now: one hour per day on these three skills. Ten years from now, you will thank yourself.
